About us

YepTennis: ATP and WTA tennis in one place.

YepTennis is an independent website for tennis fans. It brings together the day's matches, recent results, rankings, the tournament calendar and the latest tennis news, so you can follow the ATP and WTA tours from one page, on your phone or your computer.

What you'll find

  • Scores for today and the three previous days, with live scores during play.
  • ATP and WTA singles rankings, top 20, with a short profile of each player.
  • The calendar of Grand Slams and 1000, 500 and 250 tournaments.
  • Tennis news from BBC Sport and the ATP Tour.

Where the data comes from

Match data, rankings and the calendar come from the Tennis API (ATP, WTA, ITF) via RapidAPI. News comes from the public RSS feeds of BBC Sport and the ATP Tour, and each story links to the original article. Player profiles use the opening lines of their Wikipedia articles. Scores are updated several times a day, so they can be a few hours behind the official sources.

Independence

YepTennis is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to the ATP, the WTA, the ITF, the Grand Slam tournaments, the BBC or any player. Tournament and organisation names are used only to identify the events.
